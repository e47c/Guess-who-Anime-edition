# Anime Feud audit (November 2026)

The question and answer database of Anime Feud and Most Likely was reviewed and rebuilt so that answers are correct, rankings follow a stated criterion, and legitimate spellings are accepted.

## In numbers

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Boards (one ranked answer list each) | 91 | 124 |
| Questions (wordings) | 188 | 254 |
| Answers across all series | 3,811 | 5,443 |
| Classic boards / Most Likely boards | 55 / 36 | 84 / 40 |
| Series with a Classic pool of their own | 17 (not Gachiakuta) | 18 |
| Series with Most Likely duels | 17 (Gachiakuta: 1 board) | 18 |

**Review of the existing database:**
- **Reviewed:** all 91 boards and all 188 questions.
- **Corrected:** 41 boards, in their answers, rankings or wording.
  - 7 wordings were rewritten because they didn't match their answers.
  - 137 answers were removed, either because they were wrong or because they were replaced by a better-defined criterion.
  - 380 answers were added.
  - 484 answers were re-ranked.
- **Kept as they were:** 50 boards, after review. They are mostly opinion questions and anime tropes. Each now carries its ranking label.
- **Added:** 33 new boards (66 questions).

## What kind of question is it, and how is it ranked

Every board now declares its kind, and the game shows it above the question.

| Kind | Boards | What the answers are | How they're ranked | Label in the game |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Canon | 60 | Canon facts: a Hashira, a Devil Fruit, a villain of the story | Characters: AniList popularity (favourites decide the order, points follow a fixed survey-like curve). Techniques, places, items: how well-known they are (editors' estimate) | "Canon answers · ranked by AniList fans" / "… by how well-known" |
| Feats | 10 | The series' best in ONE category | Generated from the Anime Draft ratings (speed, physical strength, durability, combat skill, intelligence) | "Ranked by feats · Anime Draft ratings" |
| Power | 1 | "One of the strongest" | Canon outcomes (who beat whom), written on the board and checked | "Ranked by canon feats" |
| Opinion | 53 | Personality and scenario questions, anime tropes | Our editors' poll; never presented as canon | "Opinion · editors' poll, not canon" |

## Answer validation

**What is accepted:**
- **Names:** full names, first and last names, and the character's AniList name in its other word order ("Gokuu Son", "Krista Lenz").
- **Nicknames and titles from `js/feud/aliases.js`,** each one reviewed. Examples: Big Mom, Akainu, Kizaru, Shirohige, Kurohige, Deku, Uravity, Pitou, Barusu, Beako, Kira.
- **Romanisations:** folded automatically, so Gojou = Gojo, Kyoujurou = Kyojuro and Douma = Doma.
- **Spacing:** ignored, so "gumgum pistol" works.
- **Typos:** one is allowed, two on long answers.
- **Slightly longer answers:** accepted ("the rasengan jutsu").

**What is rejected:**
- Answers that only fit an unrelated entry.
- Sentences naming several things.
- Numbered forms that aren't the answer: "Super Saiyan 4" is not "Super Saiyan".
- A name shared by two answers ("Uchiha", "Kujo") asks the player to be more specific, with no strike.
- Aliases that are also another character's name in the same series. The game never adds these.

**A right answer that isn't on the board** gets "fits, but didn't make this board". It is still a strike; the rules are unchanged.

**`tools/feud-validate.mjs`:** 186 checks of legitimate variants and of answers that must not be accepted.

## Checks that now run on every change (`tools/feud-check.mjs`)

- **Ranking basis:** every board must state its kind and ranking basis.
- **Canon outcomes:** a "power" board may not rank anyone above a character who beat them in canon.
- **Ratings:** opinion boards about a rated quality ("least book-smart", "best strategist") may not contradict the Anime Draft ratings. This caught 10 contradictions during the audit.
- **Popularity drift:** fan-ranked boards are compared with the current AniList favourites, and a warning appears when popularity drifts.
- **Earlier checks:** these still run (ids, ambiguous names, duplicates, points, near-duplicate questions, opposites).

## Not changed

- **Gameplay is unchanged:** face-offs, play or pass, strikes, steals, scoring, duels and voting.
- **Rotation is unchanged:** cooldowns, per-room shared history and question ids. Existing histories still apply, because every existing question kept its id.
- **Anime Sketch is unchanged:** it now reads a frozen copy of the five Feud boards it borrows objects from (`js/sketch/feudwords.js`), so Feud's changes don't change Sketch's words.

## Limits (what this audit does not claim)

- **Opinion boards (53) are opinions.** They were reviewed so that every answer fits the question from the character's canon personality. Their order is still our editors' judgement, and the game says so.
- **"How well-known" is an estimate.** Ranking techniques, places and items this way is the editors' estimate; no survey data exists for it.
- **AniList favourites measure fan affection, not recognition.** So Buggy ranks above Kaido among One Piece villains.
- **The strength, speed and intelligence boards are only as accurate as the Anime Draft ratings.** Those were researched from canon feats, as described in the Anime Draft section of the README.
- **The hand-ranked "strongest" board is partly a judgement call.** 24 decisive outcomes are enforced. Where canon never settles a match-up (Shanks vs Whitebeard, Serie vs Himmel), the order is the editors' call.
- **Gachiakuta and Wind Breaker have thin canon,** so their boards stick to facts that could be confirmed.
