Anime Arcade Party, made for anime fans to come together, have fun, and enjoy some friendly competition.

Bring your fellow weebs, challenge your friends, put your anime knowledge to the test, and make some unforgettable memories along the way. Whether you're here to guess characters, compete in mini-games, or just mess around with your friends, there's always something to enjoy.

RULESET

Characters are drawn at random from every series you tick.

The host taps two players. Spectators follow both boards live but never see a secret character.

Easy = famous characters. Hard = deep cuts. Any character can be searched when guessing.

Everyone guesses at once. The 1st right guess scores as many points as there are players (8 at a full table), the next one less, and so on. Giving up = 0.

In one lap everyone is the imposter once, so 5 players on 1 lap = 5 rounds.

Classic: red, green, yellow, dark blue. Chaos adds pink, orange, purple and light blue: one colour per series, and every extra series adds its 31 cards to the deck.

Once: a special card (Freeze, Swap Hands, Ultimate, Future Sight) that has been played is out for the rest of the game. Reusable: played specials go back into the draw pile when the discard pile is reshuffled, so they can come round again.
